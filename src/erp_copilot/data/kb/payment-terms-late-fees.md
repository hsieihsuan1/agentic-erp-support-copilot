# Payment Terms and Late Fees

## Standard payment terms
Standard terms are net 30 days from the invoice date. Gold tier customers may have net 45 terms, which are shown on the customer agreement.

## Late fees
Invoices unpaid after the due date accrue a late fee of 1.5 percent per month on the outstanding balance, calculated daily. Late fees are waived once per calendar year for customers in good standing if requested in writing.

## Payment methods
Accepted methods are ACH, wire transfer and card. Card payments above 10,000 USD are not accepted online and must be made by wire.
