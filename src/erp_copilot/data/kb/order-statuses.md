# Order Fulfillment Statuses

## Status meanings
Processing means the order is being picked and packed. Shipped means the carrier has the package and a tracking number exists. Delivered means the carrier confirmed delivery. Backordered means one or more items are out of stock and the order ships when stock arrives. Cancelled means the order will not be fulfilled.

## Backorders
Backordered orders show an estimated restock date. The date can move by up to seven days. Customers may ask to split the shipment so that available items ship first.

## Tracking
Tracking numbers appear within 24 hours after an order moves to shipped. Carrier delays are not visible in the ERP until the carrier sends an exception event.
