# Tax and VAT on Invoices

## Tax calculation
Tax is calculated from the ship-to address and the product tax category. Tax exempt customers must have a valid exemption certificate on file, otherwise tax is charged.

## Correcting tax errors
A tax error on a posted invoice cannot be edited. Issue a credit memo for the full invoice and create a new corrected invoice.

## VAT invoices
VAT invoices must show the customer VAT registration number. A missing number places the invoice on hold until it is added.
