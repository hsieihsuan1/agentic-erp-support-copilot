# Returns and RMA Process

## Requesting a return
Returns must be requested within 30 days of delivery. Open a return merchandise authorization (RMA) from the order page and select a reason code. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

## Return shipping
Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item was damaged or the wrong item was sent. Prepaid labels are generated after the RMA is approved by the warehouse.

## Refund timing
After the warehouse receives and inspects the return, a credit memo is prepared within five business days.
