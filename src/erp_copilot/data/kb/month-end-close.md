# Month-End Close Support

## Close calendar
The general ledger period closes on business day three. Subledgers close on business day one. Posting to a closed period requires a controller to reopen it.

## Common posting errors
Error GL-0412 means the journal entry targets a closed period or an inactive account. Reopen the period or choose an open period. Error GL-0518 means debits and credits do not balance.

## Close support
During close, critical tickets get a one hour response. Support can request a period reopen on behalf of the customer, but the controller approves it.
