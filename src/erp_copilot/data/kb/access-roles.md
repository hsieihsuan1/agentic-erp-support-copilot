# User Access and Roles

## Requesting a role
Role requests are submitted through the access request form and need approval from the requester's manager and the module owner. Standard turnaround is two business days.

## Password and lockout
After five failed sign in attempts an account is locked for 30 minutes. Password resets are self service through the sign in page. Support never asks for a password.

## Segregation of duties
A user cannot hold both the invoice entry and invoice approval roles. Requests that create such a conflict are rejected automatically.
