# Ticket Priority and Escalation

## Priority levels
Critical means production is down or month-end close is blocked, with a response target of one hour. High means a major function is impaired, with a response target of four hours. Medium means a workaround exists, with a response target of one business day. Low means a question or minor issue, with a response target of three business days.

## When to escalate to a human
Escalate when the customer asks for a person, when the issue involves legal or compliance matters, when the customer is angry or threatens to cancel, when a financial adjustment needs approval, or when the knowledge base has no answer.

## Escalation handoff
A handoff must include a one paragraph summary, the customer identifier, related invoice, order or ticket numbers, and what has already been tried.
