# Credit Memo Policy

## When a credit memo can be issued
A credit memo can be issued for pricing errors, damaged goods, short shipments and duplicate billing. It cannot exceed the original invoice total. Credits are applied to the customer account and offset the next open invoice.

## Approval rules
Every credit memo requires human approval before it is posted. Credit memos above 1,000 USD require approval from a finance manager. The support copilot may only prepare a credit memo request; a human reviewer approves or declines it.

## Refunds
Cash refunds are not issued by support. Credits stay on account unless the customer has no open invoices, in which case finance processes a refund within ten business days.
