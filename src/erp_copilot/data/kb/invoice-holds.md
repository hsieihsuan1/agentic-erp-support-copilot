# Invoice Holds and Release

## Why invoices go on hold
An invoice is placed on hold automatically when the purchase order number on the invoice does not match the purchase order on file (PO mismatch), when the invoiced quantity differs from the received quantity (three-way match failure), or when the tax registration number is missing.

## How to release a hold
For a PO mismatch, ask the buyer to confirm the correct PO number and update it on the invoice header. Once the PO matches, the hold clears at the next matching run, which executes every hour. For a three-way match failure, the receiving team must correct the receipt or approve a tolerance exception. Customers cannot release holds themselves; a finance analyst must approve any manual release.

## Hold service level
Holds that remain unresolved for more than five business days are escalated to the accounts payable supervisor.
